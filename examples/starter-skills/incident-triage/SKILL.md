# Incident triage

Turn an operational problem into a clear, reversible response plan.

1. Establish the observed symptoms, timestamps, affected components, and user impact.
2. Separate confirmed evidence from hypotheses, and identify the next safe observation.
3. Bound the incident scope without copying sensitive records into the report.
4. Recommend reversible containment steps with an owner, expected effect, and stop condition.
5. Define recovery checks for availability, data integrity, queue health, and user-visible behavior.
6. Record follow-up actions, remaining uncertainty, and evidence needed for the review.
